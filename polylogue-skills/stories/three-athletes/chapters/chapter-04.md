## Decision

**Skill:** considering multiple viewpoints (Thinking — perspective-taking, `[fork]`).
**Kind:** perspective_fork.
**Challenge type:** group always.
**Variant:** 3 correct + 0 incorrect.
**Coin schedule:** 3 / 1 / 0 (locked, though "failed" is unreachable in this variant).
**Attempts allowed:** 2 (per schema; second attempt unused in practice for an all-correct fork).

**Group deliberation prompt** (presented when the fork lands; functions as the "no-wrong-answer" framing rather than a hint).

> Each of these is a real choice a thoughtful friend might make. There's no wrong answer here — but each path leads somewhere different. Talk through with your group what each one would mean before you choose.

**Prompt.** Ines is standing at the edge of the field. Minh is on the bench, alone. Reuben is across the field. What does Ines do?

**Choices.**

- (A) **Pull Reuben aside first.** Ines crosses to Reuben, says quietly, "We need to figure out what to do — together." Then both of them walk to the bench. *[correct — `next_chapter_id: chapter-05c` (Together). Perspective: friendship is a team effort; some things are too heavy to carry alone, even into someone else's hard moment.]*

- (B) **Walk straight to the bench.** Ines crosses to Minh, sits down next to him, and says — out loud, with Reuben within earshot — "Minh, what's going on. We're going to figure it out, but you have to tell us." *[correct — `next_chapter_id: chapter-05a` (Honest). Perspective: love means telling the truth out loud; silence becomes its own wall.]*

- (C) **Sit down next to Minh.** Ines walks to the bench, sits down beside Minh, and says nothing. She just stays. *[correct — `next_chapter_id: chapter-05b` (Slow). Perspective: presence is its own answer; some things need time, not pressure.]*

**Why all three are correct (fork legitimacy check).**

Each path represents a defensible perspective on what friendship requires when someone is hiding something. None is more virtuous than the others; each carries a real cost and a real gift.

- (A) — *team approach.* Risk: Minh might feel ganged-up-on, two-against-one. Gift: Reuben isn't left alone wondering, and Minh sees that the trio has stayed a trio.
- (B) — *direct ask.* Risk: Minh might feel cornered or exposed, with Reuben within earshot. Gift: the silence ends; Minh doesn't have to guess whether his friends still want him there.
- (C) — *patient presence.* Risk: Minh might keep carrying it alone if Ines never says anything. Gift: Minh isn't pushed before he's ready; the friendship offers room rather than demand.

A thoughtful person who valued team-effort, honesty, or patient presence would each defend their corresponding choice. The fork is legitimate.

**No abrupt-end pages** — no incorrect choice, no failure path.
