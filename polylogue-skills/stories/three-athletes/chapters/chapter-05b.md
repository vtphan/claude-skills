# Chapter 5b — *Slow* (ending after fork choice C)

**Purpose.** The group chose to sit beside Minh in silence. Time passes. Eventually, on an unrelated walk weeks later, Minh tells them — without ceremony, without it being a big deal. The friendship is the same shape, just slower. What got said by not saying did most of the work.
**Pages:** 2.
**Comprehension check:** none.
**Decision:** none.

**Perspective lens:** patient presence. Some moments aren't ready to be named yet; staying is its own answer.

## Note on this branch

Of the three endings, this one is most quietly shaped. Minh's imposter feeling is never fully named, and the friends' care is offered without demand. Some students may find this ending unsatisfying — they wanted the cathartic naming. The debrief should make clear that this is not a less-good outcome; it's a different way of holding hard things, and one that values the friend's pace over the comforter's need for resolution.
