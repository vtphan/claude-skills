## Page 1 — *The bench*

**Narration.** Ines walked to the bench. She sat down next to Minh. Reuben hung back for a beat, then crossed too and stood at the end of the bench. Then he sat down on Minh's other side.

**Dialogue.**
- Ines: "Minh. What's going on. We're going to figure it out, but you have to tell us."
- *(Long pause. Minh doesn't look up.)*
- Minh: "I got invited to try out for a select team."
- Reuben: "...and?"
- Minh: "And I was scared. If I made it, I'd be the kid who left. If I didn't make it, I'd be the kid who tried to leave. Either way I lose us."
- Ines: "You wouldn't lose us."
- Minh: "I didn't know that."
