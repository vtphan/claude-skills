## Concept (Round 1 — locked)

**Logline.** Minh gets quietly invited to try out for a regional U13 select team and tells nobody — not even his best friends Ines and Reuben, who've played together since second grade. He starts skipping their Saturday rec-league practices with vague excuses. Then Ines spots him at a different field with kids she's never seen, in a jersey she doesn't recognize.

**Theme.** Success you don't trust yourself to have earned.

**Tone.** Warm, friendship-anchored, with stakes underneath.

**Arc shape.** Setup → discovery → confrontation moment (perspective fork) → divergent endings about whether the trio comes back as the same shape.

### Skills targeted

- **Primary thinking skill:** *inferencing* — `[gate]`, group default. Minh's deflections, the second-field discovery, and the silence at lunch are all engineered to read inferentially.
- **Secondary skills (locked at Round 3 per chapter):**
  - *Distinguishing fact from opinion* — separating what Ines saw from what Ines concluded.
  - *Recognizing assumptions* — Ines's reading of betrayal vs. what Minh is actually feeling.
  - *Considering multiple viewpoints* — `[fork]`, group always. The bench-moment fork has three legitimate paths.

## Outline (Round 3 — proposal; awaiting confirmation)

Five chapters. Y-shape: Chapters 1–3 linear, Chapter 4 is the fork, Chapters 5a/b/c are the three endings (only one is read per playthrough).

### Chapter 1 — *Saturday rhythm*

**Purpose.** Establish the trio in their natural state: rec-league practice, the friendship dynamic, what Minh is normally like before anything changes. Grade-6 readers get oriented to the world.

**Pages:** 3.
**Comprehension check:** vocabulary in context — a soccer-specific word that arises naturally in dialogue (likely *"keepaway"* or *"caps off"* — confirm at draft).
**Decision:** none (linear chapter; routes to Chapter 2).

### Chapter 2 — *The orange jersey*

**Purpose.** Minh receives the select-team invitation off-screen and then must show up to Saturday practice carrying the secret. The reader sees Minh deflect his friends and process the invitation alone; the chapter ends before he can decide whether to tell.

**Pages:** 3.
**Comprehension check:** none (no natural vocab moment; chapter focused on internal tension).
**Decision:** gate, **inferencing** (primary skill). *"What's going on with Minh?"* Wrong choices encode the three failure modes from `skills-reference.md`:
- Surface-reading: "Minh is just tired today."
- Over-inferring: "Minh is being scouted by a college."
- Wrong-direction: "Minh is mad at Coach Reyna."
- Correct: "Minh is hiding something — probably something he doesn't know how to share."

Routes to Chapter 3.

### Chapter 3 — *The second field*

**Purpose.** Ines spots Minh at a different field, in a bright orange jersey, with kids she doesn't recognize. The chapter is staged for inferencing about what she saw — but the load-bearing skill at the gate is the *next* step: separating what Ines saw from what she concluded.

**Pages:** 3.
**Comprehension check:** vocabulary in context — likely *"select team"* or *"tryout"*, contextually decodable from the scene.
**Decision:** gate, **multi-detail inferencing** (primary skill, second exercise). *"Putting together what Ines saw at the second field with what's been happening at Saturday practice — what's the most supported reading?"* The correct answer requires combining at least two pieces of evidence (the absence pattern + the unfamiliar jersey + Minh's deflections). Wrong choices encode the inferencing failure modes from `skills-reference.md`: surface-reading (one detail only), over-inferring (dramatic leap), wrong-direction (fixates on weak detail).

Routes to Chapter 4.

### Chapter 4 — *Bench moment* (FORK)

**Purpose.** Minh finally shows up to Saturday practice, sits silent on the bench. The team warms up around him. Ines is at the moment of decision — what does she do?

**Pages:** 3.
**Comprehension check:** none (chapter is staged for the fork; no natural vocabulary moment that doesn't dilute the climactic feel).
**Decision:** **perspective fork** — *considering multiple viewpoints*, group always. Three correct paths, each routing to a different ending chapter:
- *(a) Confront directly* — Ines asks Minh out loud, in front of Reuben, what's been going on. Routes to Chapter 5a.
- *(b) Give space* — Ines sits down next to Minh and says nothing, just stays. Routes to Chapter 5b.
- *(c) Draw Reuben in* — Ines pulls Reuben aside first, then they approach together. Routes to Chapter 5c.

(Fork variant locked: **3 correct + 0 incorrect** — no failure mode at the fork itself, since each path is genuinely defensible.)

### Chapter 5a — *Honest* (ending after path a)

**Purpose.** Confronted directly, Minh cracks. He names the imposter feeling for the first time. The trio's understanding shifts; the friendship feels more honest, but the easy rec-league rhythm is gone.

**Pages:** 2.

### Chapter 5b — *Slow* (ending after path b)

**Purpose.** Given space, Minh eventually speaks — but slowly, over weeks. The trio remains intact, the friendship a quieter thing. The story ends without the imposter feeling fully named, just held.

**Pages:** 2.

### Chapter 5c — *Together* (ending after path c)

**Purpose.** Approached as a pair, Minh sees that his friends came as a team. The imposter feeling gets named in the context of being held. The trio acts as a unit through the difficulty going forward.

**Pages:** 2.

## Endings (for `endings` array at export)

- **chapter-05a (Honest).** Summary: After Ines walks straight to the bench and asks Minh out loud, Minh tells the trio about the select-team invitation under pressure, naming his fear that being chosen meant losing his friends. The trio is changed — more honest, less easy. Perspective lens: truth told out loud.
- **chapter-05b (Slow).** Summary: After Ines sits beside Minh in silence, the immediate moment passes without resolution. Three weeks later, on an unrelated walk to the corner store, Minh tells them about making the select team without ceremony. The trio is the same shape, quieter. Perspective lens: patient presence as its own answer.
- **chapter-05c (Together).** Summary: After Ines pulls Reuben in first and the two approach the bench together, Minh sees that his friends came as a unit. He tells them quickly, in the context of being held by both. The trio leaves the chapter more team-shaped than it started. Perspective lens: friendship as a team effort.

## Debrief (for `debrief` block at export)

**Skills practiced.** Vocabulary in context. Inferencing (single-detail and multi-detail). Considering multiple viewpoints.

**Perspective contrast.** Three friends watched a fourth carry something private. Each path the group could have taken at the bench — pulling Reuben in to approach as a team, asking Minh out loud with everyone within earshot, sitting beside him in silence — represents a real way to be a friend in a hard moment. The path your group lived through chose [chosen branch's lens]. The paths you didn't live through aren't wrong; they're different. A friend who values truth told out loud would have walked straight to the bench. A friend who values patient presence would have sat down without speaking. A friend who values being a team would have pulled Reuben in first. Each carries something the others don't. Minh got asked to try out for a select team. He kept it secret because being chosen felt like being mistaken — he didn't trust the choosing. The shape of how his friends found out shaped how he came to trust himself.

**Reflection question.** Have you ever waited too long to tell a friend something good — because telling them felt like making it real?
