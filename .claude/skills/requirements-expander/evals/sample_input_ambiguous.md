# Internal Knowledge Hub — Draft Spec

## Goal
A place where everyone at the company can find answers to "how do we do X" without having to ask in Slack.

## Scope
- Anyone can write an article.
- Articles can be searched and browsed by topic.
- Articles can be "endorsed" by trusted reviewers so readers know what's trustworthy.
- Articles expire and need to be refreshed periodically.

## Roles
- **Reader** — everyone at the company.
- **Author** — someone writing an article.
- **Reviewer** — a trusted person who can endorse articles.
- **Admin** — manages topics and reviewer permissions.

## Notes
- We haven't decided whether articles are single-owner or editable by anyone after publication.
- "Trusted reviewer" criteria are TBD.
- Refresh cadence is probably quarterly but unconfirmed.
