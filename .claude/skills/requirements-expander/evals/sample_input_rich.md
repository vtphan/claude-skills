# Bookclub Companion — Project Brief

## Goal
Give small, in-person book clubs a lightweight companion app that helps them pick books, schedule meetings, and capture the discussion afterward — without any of the social-network noise of existing "readers'" apps.

## Scope
**In scope**
- Private clubs (max ~20 members, invite-only)
- Nominating and voting on the next book
- Scheduling the next meeting (date, time, location/venue)
- Lightweight notes during the meeting (shared scratchpad)
- A per-book archive of discussion notes and ratings after the meeting

**Out of scope**
- Public discovery or social feeds
- Buying or recommending books from retailers
- Audio/video conferencing (the club meets in person)
- Reading-progress tracking or annotations inside books

## Intended user roles
- **Club organizer** — one per club, sets up the club, invites members, has tie-breaking authority on votes and scheduling.
- **Club member** — invited participant who nominates books, votes, RSVPs, and contributes notes.
- **Guest** — a one-time visitor invited to a single meeting, can view the current book and meeting details but cannot vote.

## Success metrics
- 70% of invited members attend at least one meeting in their first 2 months.
- Clubs run for at least 4 consecutive monthly meetings on average.

## Constraints
- Mobile-first (most use happens on phones).
- No required account for guests — magic-link access only.
