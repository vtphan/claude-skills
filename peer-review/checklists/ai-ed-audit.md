# AI-in-education audit

- Is the AI component central and meaningful?
- Is there a credible theory of educational benefit?
- Are learning claims supported by learning measures?
- Are simpler non-AI or weaker-AI alternatives considered?
- Are safety, equity, privacy, and integrity risks discussed concretely?
- Is the paper grounded in AIED or learning-sciences literature rather than only recent LLM work?

## Minimum sufficient pass

If speed matters, answer only these first:

- Is the AI contribution genuinely doing meaningful work?
- Are educational claims matched to the measures used?
- Is the paper grounded in the right literature tradition?
