# Theory and framework audit

- Are the key constructs defined clearly?
- Is the framework internally coherent?
- Is the contribution genuinely new, or mainly synthesis or relabeling?
- Does the framework explain or organize something that matters?
- Is it actionable for future research, design, or analysis?
- Is the literature grounding broad and foundational enough?
- Are scope conditions and limits explicit?

## Minimum sufficient pass

If speed matters, answer only these first:

- Is the framework genuinely new enough for the venue?
- Are the constructs coherent and usable?
- Does the literature grounding support the novelty claim?
