# Empirical audit

- Are the research question and contribution aligned?
- Is the design adequate for the claim strength?
- Are the measures valid for what is being claimed?
- Are analysis choices appropriate and reported clearly?
- Are effect sizes, uncertainty, or qualitative rigor visible where needed?
- Are the main threats to validity acknowledged?
- Is any core conclusion underreported, unsupported, or overclaimed?

## Minimum sufficient pass

If speed matters, answer only these first:

- Is the design adequate for the strongest claim?
- Are the measures valid for what is being claimed?
- Is the central conclusion supported, underreported, or overclaimed?
