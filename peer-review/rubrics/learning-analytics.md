# Rubric: learning analytics and educational data mining papers

Use this rubric for papers whose main contribution is analytic methodology, modeling, dataset-based insight, or data-driven intervention design.

## Contribution type

- Is the paper's main contribution methodological, empirical, theoretical, or operational?
- If it uses standard methods on a new dataset, is the educational insight strong enough to justify the paper?
- If it claims methodological novelty, is that novelty substantive?

## Data and context

- Is the dataset described clearly: population, course context, collection process, preprocessing, missingness, and exclusions?
- Are important context variables available, or are conclusions likely confounded?
- Is the scale of the data relevant to the claims being made?

## Measures and construct validity

- Do the logged variables actually measure the educational constructs the paper claims to study?
- Are proxies justified, especially for learning, engagement, collaboration, or persistence?
- Are labels, annotations, or derived variables reliable?

## Methods and analysis

- Are the modeling or analytic choices appropriate to the question?
- Are baselines strong enough?
- Are performance metrics, uncertainty, and error analysis reported?
- Is there a clear separation between exploratory and confirmatory analysis?

## Actionability and educational significance

- Does the paper produce insights that matter for instruction, support, or theory?
- If the paper predicts something accurately, does it also explain why that matters educationally?
- Are interventions or implications proportional to the evidence?

## Ethics and reproducibility

- Are privacy, consent, fairness, and surveillance concerns addressed?
- Are code, data, prompts, or synthetic reproductions shared when possible?
- Is there enough methodological transparency for others to reproduce the analysis?

## Common review pivots

Questions often worth escalating to Phase 2:

- Is the educational contribution strong enough if the method is standard?
- Are the claimed constructs valid, or are they only convenient proxies?
- Does the paper's scale meaningfully justify the venue fit?
